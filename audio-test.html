LaroFit Audio Test

Finds out whether your music can keep playing while the app listens and speaks.

How to run it

1. Earbuds in. Start SiriusXM and leave it playing.
2. Come back here and tap test 1. It runs for a few seconds.
3. Answer the question it asks about the music.
4. If the music stopped, go and restart it before the next test.
5. Do all seven, then tap Copy results and send them.

Ready. Start your music first.
