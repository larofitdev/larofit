1. Built-in speech recognition

Tap, allow the microphone, then say: “eight reps at one thirty five”.

2. Record through the microphone

Tap, then say the same phrase. It records 4 seconds and plays it back.

3. Speak through the earbuds

Tap and listen. You should hear a sentence in your earbuds.

4. Speak-then-listen experiments

Earbuds in. Tap each button once, in order, and wait for it to finish before the next. Each time, say “skip” as soon as the box below says LISTENING. Nothing to judge yourself — just copy the results at the end.
