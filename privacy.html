PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated October 13, 2026
Draft. This document has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

This policy explains what LaroFit collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. LaroFit is run by LARO Farms Inc., doing business as LaroFit (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • We collect what you give us to run your training: your account details, your profile, your workouts, programs and measurements.
  • In a Voice workout, short clips of your speech are sent to a transcription service so the app can understand you. We do not keep the audio.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from the Profile page at any time.

Voice workouts and your microphone

LaroFit only uses your microphone when you choose Voice to start a workout, and your device will ask your permission first. While a Voice workout is running:

  • The microphone stays on so the app can hear your commands. Sound is checked on your device; only short stretches that appear to contain speech are sent anywhere.
  • Those short clips are sent to a transcription service (see “Who else handles your data”) and turned into text. We do not store the audio.
  • If someone speaks near you, their words may be picked up and transcribed too. Use “pause” or end the workout when you are having a conversation.
  • The app keeps a diagnostic log of the workout: the text of what was heard, what the coach said, timings, loudness levels, the name of the microphone in use, and your browser and device type. This log is saved to your account so that voice problems can be diagnosed and fixed. It may be read by the people who build and support LaroFit.
  • The coach’s spoken lines are produced by sending their text (for example an exercise name, a weight and a number of reps) to a text-to-speech service.

LaroFit does not create a voiceprint or identify people by their voice. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated and you will be asked first.

How we use your data

  • To provide the app: storing your training, working out progression, building workouts and answering your voice commands.
  • To keep the service working: finding and fixing faults, and preventing misuse.
  • To contact you about your account, for example to verify your email or reset your password.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Where your data is kept

Your data is stored on our providers’ servers, which may be in a different country from yours. A copy of your recent data is also kept on your own device so the app loads quickly and works with a poor connection; signing out or clearing the app’s data removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, programs, workouts, measurements, custom exercises, voice logs, error reports, usage counts and sign-in details are deleted from our database. Copies in our providers’ routine backups are removed on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your data: in the app, on the Profile, Progress and Build pages.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Delete account. This cannot be undone.
  • Get a copy of your data: Profile → Download my data.
  • Ask a question: email support@larofit.com.
  • Turn off the microphone: use Manual workouts, or remove the microphone permission in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, such as objecting to certain uses of your data or complaining to a data protection authority. We will honour valid requests wherever you are.

Health and fitness information

Some of what LaroFit stores, such as your body weight, measurements and training history, may count as health information under the law where you live. We use it only to provide the app to you, as described above, and you can delete it at any time.

Children

LaroFit is not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database is set up so that each account can read only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that a breach will never happen. If one affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
