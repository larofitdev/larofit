Exercise carries risk. Please read this before you train with LaroFit.

Talk to a doctor first

Before starting this or any exercise programme, consult a doctor or other qualified health professional, especially if you are pregnant, are recovering from an injury or surgery, have a heart, lung, joint or other medical condition, take medication, or have not exercised for some time.

LaroFit is not medical advice

Nothing in LaroFit is medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or physiotherapy, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified trainer who knows you. If you are following a rehabilitation plan, follow your clinician’s instructions over anything the app suggests.

Suggestions are automated

The weights, repetitions, hold times and workouts LaroFit suggests are produced automatically, by fixed rules and by AI. They do not take account of your health, your injuries, how you feel today or your technique. They can be wrong. You are responsible for choosing what you lift and for deciding when to stop. If a suggested weight seems too heavy, use less.

Train safely

  • Learn the correct technique for an exercise before adding weight. The videos and instructions are a guide, not supervision.
  • Use equipment that is in good condition, and a spotter or safety bars where appropriate.
  • Stop immediately and seek medical help if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath.
  • In a Voice workout, keep your attention on your surroundings and your lift, not on the app.

You train at your own risk

In an emergency

LaroFit cannot call for help. In a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.
